for the globe with quantified spatial uncertainty. https://doi.org/10.5194/soil-7-217-2021

FieldShift clipped native 250 m cells around four Philippine pilot points,
retained per-depth mean/Q05/Q95 predictions, and encoded source nodata as null
in the JSON extract. The raster archive retains the source nodata value -32768.
These adaptations are not endorsed by ISRIC. Retrieval times and source URLs
are in manifest.json; retrieval is not the date of soil observation. No field
sampling, measured drainage, organic-matter conversion, or compaction diagnosis
is supplied by this extract. Model estimates require local verification.
