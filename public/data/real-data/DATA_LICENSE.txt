NASA MODIS/Terra Vegetation Indices, MOD13Q1.061:
Didan, K. (2021). MODIS/Terra Vegetation Indices 16-Day L3 Global 250m SIN Grid
V061. NASA LP DAAC. https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MOD13Q1.061
Accessed November 15, 2026 through AppEEARS 3.137. CSV values are already scaled.
The accompanying source archive retains the supplied CSV, product metadata,
granule list and README; request-public.json contains public extraction parameters
only. Account identifiers from the original request export are excluded.
Adaptations: quality screening, calendar-month aggregation and coverage checks.

NASA/USGS SRTM GL1 V003:
NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission Global 1 arc second V003.
https://doi.org/10.5067/MEaSUREs/SRTM/SRTMGL1.003
Acquired November 15, 2026 from the public Open Topo Data sampling API:
https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/
Nearest-neighbor native posts retained; percent slopes derived with a Horn 3x3
operator and local WGS84 horizontal distances. Observation: February 2000.

NASA POWER:
NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project, funded through NASA Earth Science.
https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/
Public daily point API responses acquired November 15, 2026, baseline 2014–2023.
Adaptations: complete-year climatologies, annual heat counts, preliminary threshold
screens and descriptive OLS rainfall trends. These are regional gridded estimates.
